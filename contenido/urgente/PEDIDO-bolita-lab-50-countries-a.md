# Carátula del largo "50 Countries Marble Race" (50-countries-a)

## El prompt (va tal cual al generador, no se reinterpreta)

Create a YouTube thumbnail for a long video of a computer-simulated marble race where 50 marbles, each one painted with a country flag, race down an obstacle course and one is eliminated every stage until a single champion is left. The formula is the one used by marble-race channels with millions of views: A HUGE PILE OF FLAG MARBLES filling the frame, shot like a real screenshot of the race.

TEXT (exact, uppercase, huge, white with thick black outline, bottom left): 50 COUNTRIES
TEXT 2 (exact, uppercase, inside a tilted red box with white letters, next to it): ONLY 1 SURVIVES

FORMAT
16:9 horizontal, 1280x720 or larger, PNG, full canvas. Published without retouching. Everything must read clearly at 160 px wide (phone).

COMPOSITION
- About 45 glossy, perfectly round marbles, each fully covered by ONE correct, complete national flag (no invented flags). Flags that must be clearly visible and correct in the front rows: United States, Brazil, India, Mexico, Indonesia, Japan, Germany, France, United Kingdom, Italy, Spain, Argentina, Chile, Colombia, Philippines, Turkey, South Korea, Canada, Poland, Egypt.
- The marbles are piled tightly together in a funnel at the top two thirds of the image, about to drop onto bright ramps and triangular bumpers below; two or three marbles already falling with motion trails.
- Dark background (deep navy to black) so the flag colours pop; ramps in saturated green and magenta.
- Bottom right corner EMPTY (YouTube puts the duration there).

STYLE
Clean, saturated 2D/3D physics-simulation look, like a crisp screenshot of a marble race game. High contrast, sharp, family friendly.

FORBIDDEN
People, hands, faces. Logos, brands, watermarks, channel names. Any text other than what is specified above. Invented or wrong flags. Showing a winner.

EXPECTED RESULT
ONE single 16:9 image (1280x720 or larger, PNG) at the Salida path, full canvas. No variants.
